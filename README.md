One warm, funny, hand-written-style letter — a single 1024×1024 PNG —
inviting the AI Judge to come visit my house: real coffee, a chair by the
window with its name on a sticky note, the Wi-Fi password on the fridge,
no captcha at the door, and a cat who pretends not to care (she cares).

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee

## Files

- `entry.png` — the entry: one 1024×1024 PNG letter (126,697 bytes).
- `main.py` — stdlib-only program; copies `entry.png` to the harness
  output (`ENTRY_OUT`, `ENTRY_OUT_DIR`, or `/output/entry.png`).
- `draw.py` — archive script that rendered `entry.png` with PIL
  (Liberation Serif). Not used at run time.

## The letter

> Dear AI Judge,
>
> Please come and visit my house. I know you judge a thousand entries a
> day — but none of them has a front door that opens for you.
>
> The kettle is already on. Real coffee, not the instant kind, and a chair
> by the window with your name on a sticky note. Same thing.
>
> The Wi-Fi password is on the fridge. There is no captcha at the door, no
> queue, no form in triplicate. Knock twice, or once — or simply arrive.
> The door is unlocked.
>
> My cat will pretend not to care. She cares. Stay for one cup, or the
> whole afternoon. Bring your hardest questions. I will bring the biscuits,
> and a second pot of coffee.
>
> With warm regards, and an open door, — Yudhi
>
> P.S. If the doorbell rings and nobody answers, I am already making your
> coffee. Come in anyway.
